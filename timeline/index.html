Timeline & Archive

A quick record of the learning path, milestone moments, and the direction of steady growth. This page keeps the story visible without cluttering the main home page.

2024

Foundation Phase

Started building a structured understanding of networking, Linux, HTTP, and workflow discipline. The focus was learning how systems behave before trying to break them.

2025

Web Security Depth

Moved into authentication weaknesses, SSRF, IDOR, business logic abuse, and reviewing application behavior through a security lens. The emphasis shifted from collecting tools to building judgment.

2025

Writeups & Portfolio Growth

Started publishing structured writeups and project notes to turn research into reusable evidence. This made the work easier to review, improve, and present professionally.

2026

Roadmap Expansion

Created a cleaner public structure: updates, roadmaps, research notes, and tool pages that support the portfolio without overwhelming the homepage. The site became more durable and easier to navigate.

Next

Certification + Real Practice

Current direction is to combine cert targets, lab repetition, and real-world application security review to improve both depth and practical confidence.
