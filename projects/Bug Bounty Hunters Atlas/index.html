NPX's Hunting Strategy

When picking a package to audit, I look for this specific combination:

  • Decent install numbers (popular enough to be used).
  • Last commit two or three years ago (abandoned by the original author).
  • It does something scary like file uploads, auth, deserialization or shelling out.

"That combo hits more often than it has any right to."
