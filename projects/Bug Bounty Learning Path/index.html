⚠️ Warning

This path alone is not enough to become a professional Bug Bounty Hunter.
It's just a roadmap — success requires:

  • Deep understanding of HTTP, JavaScript, APIs, Linux
  • Daily practice (6–8 months minimum)
  • Strong reconnaissance methodology
  • Reading and analyzing others' reports
  • Patience and persistence (success doesn't happen overnight)

🔍 Phase 5: Reconnaissance

🎯 Recon is 70% of success ⏱️ 1–2 months
Passive ReconSubfinder, Amass, Certificate Transparency
Active ReconNmap, HTTP probing, directory enumeration
Wayback MachineHistorical URLs and endpoints
GitHub DorkingFind secrets in public repos

🧠 Recon Mindset

  • Map the attack surface before testing
  • Find hidden endpoints and subdomains
  • Identify technologies and versions
  • Prioritize high-value targets

🧠 Phase 6: Real World

🎯 Transition to real-world application ⏱️ Ongoing
Read Bug Bounty ReportsAnalyze reports from professionals
Practice on Private ProgramsJoin VDP programs
Join HackerOne / BugcrowdStart with open programs
Write Professional ReportsLearn to write convincing reports
Be Patient & PersistentFirst bounty may take months

📝 Report Structure

  • Title: Concise and impactful
  • Description: Detailed vulnerability info
  • Steps to Reproduce: Clear instructions
  • Proof of Concept: Screenshots or videos
  • Impact: Real-world consequences

🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

Rushing into bounty programsMaster the fundamentals first
Ignoring reconnaissanceRecon = 70% of success
Over-relying on toolsUnderstand what the tools do
Writing unclear reportsGood report = Better payout
Giving up after rejectionRejection is part of the journey

Beginners Roadmap to Bug Bounty Hunting

Greetings! I'm Lalatendu Swain, a Security Engineer and part-time content creator. I've initiated this repository to provide guidance to aspiring bug bounty hunters. Content will be continually added, so stay tuned and let's embark on this journey together!

Please Note: Bug bounty landscapes have evolved significantly in recent years. The vulnerabilities that were once easily discovered may now pose greater challenges due to increased automation and competition. To succeed in bug bounty hunting, persistence and focus are key.
